# Group passports: see how the whole group is doing

**Group progress**

A shared passport edition now shows how the whole group is doing: how many airfields have been visited by at least one participant and by everyone, and a leaderboard of each participant's airfields and coverage. Tied participants share a place.

- Everyone is measured against the same airfields: all open ones, plus closed ones that someone visited. A closed airfield nobody reached is left out for everyone, and the page says how many.
- New filters show the airfields visited by anyone, by everyone or by nobody, or by one particular participant. Your own progress and marker styles always stay yours.
- The figures cover the friends sharing their visits in that edition, and update straight away when someone joins, leaves or is removed. They're counts only; nobody's flights or notes are shown.
